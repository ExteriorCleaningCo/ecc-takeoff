How it works

Set the scale first (S). Pick a group, then click the corners of an area. Double-click, press Enter or click the first point to finish. Hold Shift for straight lines. Scroll or two-finger drag to move, pinch or hold Ctrl/⌘ and scroll to zoom. Space drags the drawing. ⌘Z undoes.

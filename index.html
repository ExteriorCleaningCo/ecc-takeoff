How it works

1. Start a New job and fill in the site and customer details (rates and terms can wait until the end, see Rates & terms). 2. Add a PDF and set its scale. Use + Add PDF for each further drawing: everything already measured stays. 3. Add a house type, give it its plot numbers, then pick what you are measuring (Brickwork, Render and so on) and click round it. Use Cut-out for windows and doors. 1–5 switch the area, Enter finishes a shape, Space drags the drawing, ⌘Z undoes. 4. Export… makes the Excel files, the PDF and the covering letter.

How it works

1. Click Job details and fill in the site, client and your rates. 2. Set the drawing scale. 3. Add a house type, give it its plot numbers, then pick what you are measuring (Brickwork, Render and so on) and click round it. Use Cut-out for windows and doors. 1–5 switch the area, Enter finishes a shape, Space drags the drawing, ⌘Z undoes. 4. Export to Excel makes your tender summary.
